# Diné / Navajo — research dossier

**People:** Diné (Navajo Nation).  
**Do not collapse into:** “Southwestern Native sky lore.”

## Public educational sources (community-linked)

- Nancy C. Maryboy and David Begay, *Sharing the Skies: Navajo Astronomy*
  (Indigenous Education Institute / World Hope Foundation, 2010).
  Smithsonian catalog: https://www.si.edu/object/siris_sil_941305
  Community-authored / Diné-led educational book. Names public
  educational asterisms including Dilyéhé (Pleiades), Átsé Ets’ózí
  (Orion), Náhookǫs Bi’ka’ / Bi’áád / Biko’ (Ursa Major, Cassiopeia,
  Polaris).
- NASA and the Navajo Nation educator guide *Story of the Stars*
  (Window Rock meeting 2005; delivered 2006 to Navajo Nation schools).
  https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/education/nasa-and-the-navajo-nation/story-of-the-stars/
  Dual-learning STEM/cultural classroom material.
- *Navajo Skies* planetarium show and teacher guide (Indigenous
  Education Institute / Sharing the Skies). Educational licensing.
- STARLAB Navajo Skies cylinder curriculum, Natural History Museum
  of Utah: https://nhmu.utah.edu/sites/default/files/attachments/D.10.NavajoSkies.pdf
- Diné College / Navajo Nation Museum STARLAB visit reported by
  *Navajo Times*, 2 Feb 2012
  (https://navajotimes.com/education/2012/0212/020212stars.php).
  Public mention of Pleiades as a planting-season marker.

## Stellarium pack — not used as primary source

Stellarium `navajo` is based on Don Childrey, *Star Trails-Navajo*,
contributed by Digitalis Education Solutions. References include
dead Clark Foundation pages and a generic “North American mythology”
Windows to the Universe page. Classification: **modern reconstruction
of a secondary book**, not a Diné-authored pack.

## Seasonal / restricted rule

STARLAB / Navajo Skies educational materials state that Diné sky
stories — including stories of the Sun, Moon, and stars — are
told in winter (first frost / October through first thunder /
late February–March), with a limited midsummer window. They are
**not culturally appropriate** as year-round public retellings.

Cartography is a persistent, season-agnostic app. That is enough
to exclude Diné constellation stories from this release.

`deferred_restricted = true`  
Reason: seasonal-only / ceremonial-protocol sky stories; year-round
digital retelling is not appropriate even when classroom materials
exist.

## Three Sisters

No Diné source reviewed uses “Three Sisters” as an authentic Diné
star name. Orion’s Belt as English “Three Sisters” is a later
European folk label and is not applied here.

## Implementation recommendation
