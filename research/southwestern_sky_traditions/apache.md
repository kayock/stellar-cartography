# Apache traditions — research dossier

Apache peoples are not one sky system. Sources were checked
separately where possible.

## Communities distinguished

- Western Apache
- Chiricahua
- Mescalero (Nde)
- Jicarilla
- Lipan

## Sources reviewed

- Claire R. Farrer, “Star Clocks: Mescalero Apache Ceremonial Timing,”
  *Canadian Journal of Native Studies* 7(2).
  https://cjns.brandonu.ca/wp-content/uploads/7-2-farrer.pdf
  Documents star timing in the **girls’ puberty ceremonial**
  (Big Dipper / nakakus; Capella as morning-star check).
  Classification: scholarly ethnographic. **Ceremonial / restricted.**
- Mescalero Apache Cultural Center & Museum (tribal site).
  https://mescaleroapachetribe.com/mescalero-apache-cultural-center-museum/
  Public museum of material culture. No public constellation curriculum
  sufficient for a story card.
- Jicarilla Apache Cultural Arts & Heritage Center.
  https://jicarilla-culturalart.org/
  Arts and heritage. No public star-asterism pack found.
- Museum of Indian Arts and Culture / Museum of New Mexico Foundation,
  *Makowa* exhibition announcement (2025), listing Jicarilla Apache
  Nation and N’de (Mescalero Apache) among communities whose sky
  knowledge the exhibit explores.
  https://www.museumfoundation.org/eyes-on-the-sky-exhibition-highlights-indigenous-astronomy/
  Useful as a pointer. Exhibition text was not available in a form
  that could be excerpted into Cartography without community review.

## Findings

The only detailed published star-timing account found (Farrer) is
tied to a living ceremony. Phase 4 forbids placing that in
Cartography.

No Western Apache, Chiricahua, Jicarilla, or Lipan public
educational asterism set of READY quality was found.

## Three Sisters

No Apache-community source reviewed documents a celestial
“Three Sisters” grouping.

## Implementation recommendation

**DEFER / RESTRICTED.**

`deferred_restricted = true` for Mescalero ceremonial star-clock
material (puberty ceremonial timing).  
Other Apache communities: **RESEARCH_MORE** until a specific
community publishes public educational sky material.

## Deepening pass

Jicarilla Cultural Arts & Heritage Center was revisited: arts
and heritage, no reusable star-name curriculum. Great Sand Dunes
hosts a “Jicarilla Apache Star Stories” video; it is not a
citable public educational text for Cartography. Western Apache
public planetarium/STARLAB materials were not found. Status
unchanged: no Apache production stories.
