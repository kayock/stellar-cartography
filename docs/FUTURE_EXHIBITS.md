## Forgotten Contributions

### Purpose

A historically sourced exhibit highlighting **Soviet achievements and
contributions** in astronomy, spaceflight, robotic exploration, and planetary
science.

The exhibit is intended to surface important scientific and engineering work
that is often reduced or omitted in simplified Cold War narratives.

### Status

**Not implemented.** Planning / research scope only. Do not begin build work
from this document alone.

### Potential areas for future research / exhibit development

These are **topic prompts for future research**, not verified exhibit copy:

- Sputnik and early artificial satellites
- Luna program
- First spacecraft to reach / impact the Moon
- First images of the lunar far side
- First soft landing on the Moon
- Lunokhod robotic lunar rovers
- Yuri Gagarin / first human spaceflight
- Valentina Tereshkova / first woman in space
- Voskhod milestones
- Salyut and Mir space-station heritage
- Venera / Venus atmospheric and surface exploration
- Mars probe efforts where scientifically relevant
- Planetary-science instrumentation and data
- Astronomical observatories / astrophysics contributions
- Robotic-engineering advances
- International / scientific legacy where appropriate

### Required standards before any implementation

Any future Forgotten Contributions build must use:

- Authoritative historical and scientific sources
- Primary or institutional sources where possible
- Careful distinction between **achievement**, **propaganda**, **uncertainty**,
  and **failure**
- Accurate dates and mission outcomes
- Provenance for all media (same honesty standard as existing Specials)

Avoid political advocacy. Focus on historical and scientific accuracy.
